01 — The practice

One person, the whole build.

"A client rarely needs a website. They need bookings that don't get missed, payments that land clean, and a back office someone can actually run."

The client list is mostly local: tour operators and travel agents across Kutch, jewellers and clothing brands, furniture and logistics firms, a security company, a newspaper. Practical software for businesses that run on it daily, not portfolio pieces.
